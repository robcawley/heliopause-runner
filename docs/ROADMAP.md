# Roadmap

Each stage ends with something playable and a note in FEEL-LOG.md.

0. **Feel prototype** (now). Cockpit, belt, near miss, phase, tuning panel. Exit test: thirty seconds
   of empty belt is fun.
1. **Weapons.** Auto-fire from the cockpit, reticle, bolts with light and sound, rocks that break.
2. **The Director.** Port v1's wave logic; Scouts first, then Interceptors and Heavy Raiders.
   Enemy bolts designed for first-person readability.
3. **Upgrades as modules.** The draft becomes three modules slotting into the dash.
4. **The garden and the story.** Drifters as bioluminescent creatures, MICA and the chapters as comms.
5. **Art pass.** Real cockpit model, enemy designs, music integration.
6. **Ship it.** Build, upload to robcawley.com alongside v1.
