# Heliopause Runner: The Belt — the release package

Decided 2 Oct 2026 with Rob: narrow the game to a complete, releasable package built from what
already exists. No new big systems; finish, polish and give it an ending.

**Platform:** desktop web browser (WebGPU with the WebGL fallback). **Length:** 2 to 3 hours.

## In (exists: finish and polish)

- Flight and combat: the fighter, blasters, phase drive, rolls, PIP (Mk I and Mk II).
- Four playable regions: Beacon Lanes, Cold Nursery, Foundry Storm, the Narrows; nine towns.
- Dead Zones, the four raider clans, Carrion.
- Career: contracts, the board, the navigation chart, ferry flights, standing and locked towns.
- Market upgrades; crew, the wing, loyalty, permanent losses, radio voices.

## Needed to make it a package

1. **The ending** (below). The career currently never finishes.
2. **A first-run tutorial**: one guided contract covering controls, the lane, blasters, the board.
3. **Menus and settings**: volume, controls, invert, reduced motion, new game / continue, saves.
4. **A balance pass over a full playthrough**: credits, prices, clan threat, crew loss rate.
5. **Compatibility and QA**: WebGL fallback, main browsers, full playthrough tests.
6. **Release**: where it lives (Rob's site, itch.io, or both).

## Out (later, if ever)

Pilot rank and licences, ship classes, warlord hunts as separate content, customs scanning and
smuggling, rival smugglers, the Outer Patrol Zone, alien space beyond the ending, mobile flight,
blockchain.

## The ending (Rob, 2 Oct 2026)

**Exit into open space and find a godlike entity that warns humanity to stay in their region.**

Rob's direction is the line above. The rest is a first proposal for him to react to:

- **Unlock.** Earn real standing across the belt (Trusted with a town of each power). The Kindred
  then make contact: the humans who came back from the edge changed. They are the ones the
  entity already sent home. Arrokoth stops being uncharted.
- **The crossing.** A final contract with no lane and no beacons. The rock thins out to nothing,
  the stars shift, the crew's radio breaks up, PIP goes quiet, the music falls away to near
  silence. Open space.
- **The entity.** Vast, slow, beyond the scale of anything in the belt. Not a fight; there is
  nothing to shoot that matters. It speaks once, in a deep layered voice: humanity has its
  region; stay in it. Push on and you are turned gently back, however you fly.
- **Look (Rob, 2 Oct 2026).** A slowly turning geometry of light with an eye-like centre.
- **Words.** Inspired by the idea of boundary stones a neighbour must not move, without quoting
  scripture. Drafts in progress; Rob chooses.
- **After.** The way home, an epilogue for the crew who made it (and the fallen), and the chart
  redrawn with the heliopause as a boundary. Credits.
