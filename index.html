Runner

Flight prototype. The belt run, from the cockpit.

Click the view to take the controls: the mouse flies the ship and eases back to centre on its own. Left click fires the blasters.
WASD also flies. Hold Q or E to roll; double-tap Q, E, A or D to barrel roll. Shift boosts, X brakes, Space phases, F also fires.
Stray from the lane and PIP pops up: press R and it flies you home. R also rescues you any time you lose your bearings.
T opens the tuning panel. M mutes. P or Esc pauses.
