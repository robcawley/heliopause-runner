Flight prototype. The belt run, from the cockpit.

Fly with the mouse (the further from centre, the harder you turn), WASD, a gamepad, or a thumb on touch.
Q and E, or double-tap A or D, to barrel roll. Shift to boost, X to brake, Space to phase.
The beacon lane shields you. Outside it, radiation builds slowly, then starts to eat the hull.
T opens the tuning panel. M mutes. P pauses.
